Topic 38: Listening Comprehension

Catch the main idea, identify details, understand expressions, and check what you heard.

1. Four listening skills

Listen for gist

What is the speaker mainly talking about?

Focus on the central message, not every word.

Listen for details

Names, dates, times, numbers, locations.

Preview questions so you know what to listen for.

Infer meaning

Use tone, context, and key words.

Distinguish what is stated from what is only suggested.

Check understanding

“So the deadline is Friday, right?”

Paraphrase or ask politely when unsure.

2. Listen for signpost words

Reason: because, due to
Contrast: however, although
Result: so, therefore, as a result
Sequence: first, next, finally

3. Common spoken expressions

Give me a hand = help me

Put off = postpone

Get back to you = reply later

Running behind = delayed or late

Not my cup of tea = not something I like

Take care of it = handle it

4. If you miss a word

  1. Keep listening for the overall meaning.
  2. Use the surrounding words to infer what you missed.
  3. Ask for repetition if the detail matters.
  4. Replay a short section during practice, and note the phrase you missed.

Listening Lab: read-aloud practice

This offline simulator provides scripts and comprehension questions; it does not play audio. Read the script aloud, or use your browser's read-aloud feature if available. Then hide or scroll past the script and answer from memory.

Daily 10-minute listening routine

  1. Preview (1 minute): read the topic or questions.
  2. First listen (2 minutes): focus on the overall message.
  3. Second listen (3 minutes): note important words, numbers, and transitions.
  4. Check (2 minutes): compare with a transcript or answer key.
  5. Repeat (2 minutes): imitate one useful phrase and practise its rhythm.
