PRACTICAL ENGLISH • TOPIC 41

Vocabulary, Synonyms, Antonyms & Word Choice

Build a useful vocabulary and choose words that sound natural in everyday and professional English.

1. Synonyms and antonyms

Synonyms = similar meaning

happy → joyful
begin → start
reliable → dependable

Antonyms = opposite meaning

ancient ↔ modern
expand ↔ shrink
temporary ↔ permanent

Synonyms are not always interchangeable in every sentence. Check tone, context, and common word partners.

2. Choose words for the situation

Casual

“I got your message.”

Professional

“I received your message.”

Precise

Instead of “very tired,” say “exhausted.”

Use the simplest word that clearly expresses your meaning. Longer words are not automatically better.

3. Learn collocations

Collocations are words that commonly go together. Learning them makes your English sound more natural.

make a decision
make progress
make a plan
do homework
do a favour
do your best
take a break
take notes
take responsibility
pay attention
heavy rain
strong coffee

4. Commonly confused words

Affect is usually a verb: “The weather affects my mood.”

Effect is usually a noun: “The change had a positive effect.”

Advice is a noun: “Can you give me advice?”

Advise is a verb: “I advise you to practise daily.”

Interested describes how a person feels; interesting describes the thing that creates interest.

Word Lab

Practise building vocabulary through meaning, context, and word partners.

1. Pick a stronger or more precise word

“I was very tired after the long journey.”

2. Complete a collocation

“Please ___ attention during the explanation.”

3. Make your own vocabulary card

Daily 10-minute vocabulary routine

  1. Choose 3 words from something you read or hear.
  2. Learn meaning and pronunciation.
  3. Write one example sentence for each word.
  4. Learn a word partner such as “make a decision.”
  5. Review yesterday's words and use at least one in a conversation.
Vocabulary card example
Word: reliable
Meaning: dependable; able to be trusted
Sentence: “We need a reliable internet connection for the meeting.”
Word partner: reliable source / reliable service

A few words used correctly are more valuable than a long list you cannot remember.
