English Learning · Topic 47

Final Comprehensive English Assessment

Review grammar, vocabulary, punctuation, reading and listening strategies, speaking fluency, and professional writing in one final practice test.

How to use this assessment

  1. Take the test without looking up answers if you want to measure your current level.
  2. Choose one answer for each question and check the explanation.
  3. Review missed questions and practise those topics again.

This is a learning assessment, not an official language certificate or a complete measure of speaking and listening ability.

Skills covered

Grammar

Tenses, articles, prepositions, clauses, conditionals, voice, and reported speech.

Vocabulary

Word meaning, synonyms, antonyms, collocations, and phrasal verbs.

Reading & listening

Main ideas, summaries, context, and key details.

Speaking & writing

Conversation, professional tone, paragraph structure, and writing process.

Suggested score guide

45–50: Strong performance on this practice set.

35–44: Good foundation; review missed topics.

25–34: Keep practising core rules and examples.

0–24: Revisit earlier lessons and retake the assessment later.

These bands are informal practice guidance, not official proficiency levels.

Comprehensive test

One question at a time. Each answer includes an explanation.

Personal review plan

After your test, tick the areas you want to revise.

Suggested routine: Pick one weak area, study for 10 minutes, complete 5–10 practice questions, and write or say three original examples.

Final writing challenge

Write 100–150 words about a goal you want to achieve. Include your goal, why it matters, two steps you will take, and a conclusion.
