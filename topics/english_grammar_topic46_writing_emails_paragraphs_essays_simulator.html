English Writing: Emails, Paragraphs & Essays

Learn to organise ideas, write clear professional emails, build paragraphs, and develop essays with a strong structure.

1. Build a clear paragraph

Topic sentence

Introduce the main idea.

Supporting details

Give reasons, facts, or examples.

Closing sentence

Reinforce the idea or connect to the next point.

Example: “Daily reading improves English skills. It introduces new vocabulary in context and shows how sentences are organised. Even ten minutes of reading each day can provide useful practice.”

2. Write a professional email

  1. Use a specific subject line.
  2. Greet the reader politely.
  3. State the purpose early.
  4. Include only relevant details.
  5. Make the requested action and deadline clear.
  6. Close politely and include your name.
Subject: Request for Project Update

Hi Priya,

Could you share the latest project status by Wednesday? I need the update to prepare Friday’s release plan.

Thank you,
Raj

3. Essay structure

Introduction

Introduce the topic and state your main argument (thesis).

Body paragraphs

Each paragraph develops one point with support.

Conclusion

Bring the argument together without adding an unrelated new point.

Before writing, make a quick outline. After drafting, revise the ideas and order; proofread grammar and spelling at the end.

4. Linking words

Add

Furthermore, also, in addition

Contrast

However, although, on the other hand

Result

Therefore, as a result, consequently

Example

For example, for instance

Order

First, next, finally

Conclude

In conclusion, to sum up

5. Common mistakes to avoid

  • Run-on sentences: separate complete ideas with correct punctuation.
  • Sentence fragments: express a complete thought.
  • Repetition: combine similar ideas and remove unnecessary words.
  • Vague claims: use specific details or examples.
  • Incorrect tone: adapt language to your reader and purpose.
