Topic 33: Real-Life English Practice

Use grammar naturally in conversations, messages, and professional situations.

1. Make everyday English sound natural

Ask politely

“Could you help me with this?”

Use Could you…? for polite requests.

Clarify

“Sorry, could you explain that again?”

Ask instead of pretending you understood.

Disagree respectfully

“I see your point, but I think…”

Acknowledge the other person first.

Admit uncertainty

“I’m not completely sure, but I’ll check.”

Be clear about what you will do next.

2. Useful grammar patterns

Would you mind + -ing: “Would you mind checking this?”

Look forward to + -ing: “I look forward to hearing from you.”

Explain something to someone: “Could you explain this to me?”

For a duration / since a starting point: “for two hours” / “since 9 a.m.”

Future time clause: “I’ll call you when I arrive.”

3. Professional message formula

Greeting → Reason → Specific request or update → Thanks/sign-off

Example: “Hi Priya, I’m following up on the report. Could you share the latest version by 3 p.m.? Thanks for your help. Best regards, Aman”
