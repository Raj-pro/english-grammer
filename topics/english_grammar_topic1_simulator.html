📘 Quick guide

What is grammar?

Grammar is the set of rules that helps us put words together correctly.
Correct: She likes music.
Incorrect: She like music.

Word, phrase, sentence

Word: one unit of language — book.
Phrase: a group of words that works together but is not a complete sentence — the big book.
Sentence: a complete thought — The big book fell.

Subject + Verb + Object

Riya reads books

Subject: who/what the sentence is about.
Verb: the action or state.
Object: who/what receives the action. Not every sentence needs an object: Birds fly.

Three useful sentence forms

Statement: You are ready.
Question: Are you ready?
Negative: You are not ready.
