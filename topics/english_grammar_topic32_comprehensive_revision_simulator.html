Common error clinic

Subject–verb agreement

Incorrect: The list of items are long.

Correct: The list of items is long.

The head subject is “list,” not “items.”

Articles

Incorrect: She is a honest person.

Correct: She is an honest person.

Choose a/an by the initial sound.

Tense

Incorrect: I have seen him yesterday.

Correct: I saw him yesterday.

A finished past-time marker usually takes simple past.

Embedded questions

Incorrect: I know where is he.

Correct: I know where he is.

Use statement word order inside embedded questions.

Prepositions

Incorrect: It depends of the weather.

Correct: It depends on the weather.

Sentence boundaries

Incorrect: It was late, we left.

Correct: It was late, so we left.

Join complete clauses with a conjunction, semicolon, or full stop.
