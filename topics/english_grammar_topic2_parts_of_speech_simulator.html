🧠 Eight parts of speech at a glance

Noun Names a person, place, thing or idea. teacher, Delhi, pencil, honesty
Pronoun Replaces a noun. I, you, he, she, it, we, they
Verb Shows an action or state. run, think, is, have
Adjective Describes a noun or pronoun. blue sky, kind person
Adverb Often describes a verb, adjective or another adverb. run quickly, very tall
Preposition Shows relationships such as place, time or direction. in, at, under, toward
Conjunction Connects words or groups of words. and, but, because
Interjection Expresses a sudden feeling or reaction. Wow! Ouch! Oh!

A word's role depends on the sentence. For example, “light” is a noun in “Turn on the light” and an adjective in “a light bag.”
