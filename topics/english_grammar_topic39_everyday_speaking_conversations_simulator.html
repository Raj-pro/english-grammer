Everyday Speaking & Conversations

Start conversations, ask questions, express opinions, and sound polite and natural.

1. A simple conversation pattern

Open

“Hi! How's your day going?”

Respond

“Pretty well, thanks.”

Follow up

“What have you been working on?”

Close

“It was nice talking to you.”

Listen, respond to what the other person said, and ask a relevant follow-up question.

2. Make your language polite

Direct: “Send me the report.”

Polite: “Could you please send me the report?”

Direct: “Repeat that.”

Polite: “Sorry, could you say that again?”

Direct: “I want to ask something.”

Polite: “May I ask a quick question?”

3. A reliable speaking formula

Point → Reason → Example → Question

“I prefer studying in the morning because I feel more focused. For example, I can finish difficult tasks before lunch. When do you prefer to study?”

Useful phrases by situation

Starting a conversation

“Hi, how are you?” · “How's your week going?” · “Is this seat taken?”

Asking for clarification

“Could you explain what you mean?” · “Would you mind speaking a little more slowly?”

Sharing an opinion

“In my opinion…” · “The way I see it…” · “I think this could work because…”

Agreeing or disagreeing politely

“I see what you mean.” · “That's a fair point.” · “I understand, but I see it differently.”

Making suggestions

“Why don't we…?” · “How about trying…?” · “We could consider…”

Buying thinking time

“Let me think for a moment.” · “That's an interesting question.”

Closing a conversation

“It was nice talking to you.” · “I'd better get going.” · “Let's catch up again soon.”
